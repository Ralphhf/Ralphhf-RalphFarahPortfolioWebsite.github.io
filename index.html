New Brunswick, NJ

I build the reporting stack a commercial insurance carrier runs on — SSIS packages that load broker and policy files, T-SQL stored procedures that calculate before anything lands, SSRS reports finance and underwriting drive with their own parameters, and Power BI models on the same governed data.

Between every source and destination there is a reconciliation check — row counts and column sums that fail the load on a mismatch. It's the difference between a report that is populated and one that is correct.

Outside work I build complete systems that run without me — scrapers, pipelines, scoring engines and the interfaces on them. I build them with Claude, and in most of them Claude is also a working part of the system: classifying, extracting, reading photographs, writing the copy. Knowing where a model belongs in a system, and where it doesn't, is most of the value.
